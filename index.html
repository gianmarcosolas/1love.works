About the Project

Law is Love · Tradition → Innovation · E = MC²  →  Λ = C²  →  Λ = E / M  (JLMI 2/25)
1 The Law

The Law is like a Net

It was created to help humans not crash in moments of free fall.

2 The Problem

We "disordered" it

In the lack of technology to measure it, we put one law above the other for centuries - as a result of which a substantial amount of value remains "trapped" under the law.

3 The Solution

We are "reordering" it

We measure legal weight and entropy and engineer sustainable legal systems to unlock and distribute this value to anyone that contributes to the Project: participate.
