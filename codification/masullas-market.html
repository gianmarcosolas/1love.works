Masullas Internal Market

A closed marketplace for Masullas, denominated in MasullasCoin — the same PNC certificate's value, now also usable to buy and sell goods and services locally.

Founding Codification Agents (PNC holders) start with a balance equal to what they put in. Everyone else joins by invitation from someone already in, and starts at zero — earning coin by selling something real.

Most of what a small town spends leaves it immediately — paid to outside suppliers, chains, or services with no local presence. Coin that can only be spent inside Masullas can't leak out that way: it has to circulate locally first. That circulation is measurable, and it's exactly what the index below tracks.

1 PNC (€99) = 99 MasullasCoin, minted once at registration. MasullasCoin is a closed-loop unit of account for this prototype only — it is not redeemable for euros, not a financial instrument, and not tradable outside this market.
