II · Beyond Sardinia — EU-27

27 member states.
One untapped market.

The same λ model, applied at member-state scale, gives a first estimate of the unexpressed economic value held inside each EU country's legal and territorial systems. This is the same kind of value found in Sardinia — frozen, unmeasured, waiting to be codified — but at the scale of an entire nation.

These are starting hypotheses, not validated totals: each country's λ figure is to be confirmed empirically as codification proceeds — and the real number could turn out lower, or could uncover far more than the model suggests. For an independent reference point, a macro estimate (Eurostat-derived, JLMI 2/25) puts unexpressed value across EU-27 legal systems at roughly €5.2 trillion — see the Problem page. Potential is the size of each country's internal market once its legal entropy approaches zero; measured is what has actually been claimed and codified so far.
