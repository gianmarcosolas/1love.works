Questions
What is a scan?
A scan is the submission of one legal document — a contract, regulatory filing, corporate act, or policy — to the EU Law Scanner or Corporate Scanner. The instrument measures its λ score, entropy level, legal weight, inertia, motive power, and investability against EU law and UN SDG benchmarks.
What does PNC actually give me today?
Today, PNC is a founding certificate — proof of your participation in the first phase of the network. It records your entity's engagement with the legal-physics framework. When the blockchain layer is live (Phase 2), PNC will be recorded on-chain as a verifiable entropy reduction certificate. Founding holders receive priority access to the full network.
Why is 1 PNC = €99?
The founding price is set at the cost of the minimum service unit — one scan package. It is not a market price and not a promise of value. It is a founding-phase anchor: the price at which the first nodes enter the network before entropy measurement is live and before the market can price a quantum of legal entropy reduction.
Is PNC a financial instrument?
No. PNC is issued as part of a service transaction, not sold independently. It carries no promise of financial return and is not classified as a security or investment instrument under MiCA or any other regulatory framework. It is a mathematical certificate of service delivery and legal system measurement.
What is a Founding Node?
Founding Nodes are the first institutions to commission a full system codification — cities, municipalities, corporations, or regulators whose entire legal system is mapped, measured, and codified with the legal-physics methodology. They receive 150 PNC, named partner status, and priority access to the full network when it launches.
